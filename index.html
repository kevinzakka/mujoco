Gear backlash

An interactive model of geartrain lost motion: the clearance between meshing teeth that lets the input rotate while the output stays fixed, until the play is taken up and contact resumes.

The bench

Drag the motor. Watch the output freeze on every reversal. Set the gap to 0 for the ideal, no-slop case.

J1 — drive
The motor shaft. The commanded position, after the gearbox. This is what a motor-side encoder reads. The actuator acts here.
J2 — play
The gear clearance. Not a real pivot — a fictitious free joint that lumps the transmission's slop, bounded to |J2| ≤ gap/2. The lost motion.
J1 + J2 — output
The true link angle. Where the finger segment actually points; what a joint-side encoder reads. The motor only knows J1, so the play is hidden.
